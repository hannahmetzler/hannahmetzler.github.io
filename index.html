I currently work as a psychologist in a psychosocial rehabilitation center, and am pursuing a postgraduate degree to become a clinical psychologist & therapist. Until recently, I worked as a psychological scientist and project lead at the Complexity Science Hub and Medical University of Vienna (publications under the name Hannah Metzler). My research focused on the area of social media and psychology. I used experiments and data science to investigate misinformation spreading, suicide prevention, and emotional wellbeing. I have a background in psychology and social and affective neuroscience.

I am part of the Open Science and the Effective Altruism community and strive to increase the transparency, reproducibility, and social impact of my work.
